1. !DOCTYPE HTML

You'll need this tag at the beeginning of every HTML document you create. it ensures that a
browser knows that it's reading HTML, and that it expects HTML5, that latest version.
Even though this isn't actually an HTML tag, it's still a good one to know.

2.HTML

This is another tag that tells a browser that it's reading HTML. The tag gose
strainght after the DOCTYPE tag, and you close it with a tag right at the end of your file.
Everything else in your document goes between these tags.

3.HEAD

the tag start the header section of your file. The stuff that goes in here doesn't appear on your webpage. Instead,it contains metedata for search engines, and info for your browser.
